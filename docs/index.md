# ubrain manual

**ubrain** is an untrained brain: it learns from experience by local plasticity, and
plugs into trained AI models as senses and into bodies as its interface.

- [Quickstart](quickstart.md) -- install and run in five minutes
- [Using ubrain in your own code](using.md) -- install from the wheels, write your own
- [Concepts](concepts.md) -- regions, wiring, bodies, codecs and certificates
- The examples (`examples/`) -- short, meant to be copied, all on the whole brain; each ends
  with its check

This manual is for ubrain 0.46.0.dev3: the whole brain, `Brain()`, with seventeen regions, and the
MuJoCo G1 as its body. How the engine behind it is built is the subject of *The
Untrained Brain Series*, Volume 2: *Building the Brain Engine*.
